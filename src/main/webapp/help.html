Sunrise Dental - User Guide

  1. Authentication: Use your authorized staff credentials (Username: admin, Password: admin123) to access the system securely.
  2. Register Appointment: Navigate to the Registration tab. An appointment ID is auto-generated for convenience. Select a Doctor and Treatment from the dynamically managed lists, then click "Save".
  3. Manage Clinic Data: Navigate to "Manage Clinic" to seamlessly add or delete Doctors and Services. This will instantly update the options available in the Registration dropdowns.
  4. Billing & Receipt: Navigate to "Search & Billing". Enter an Appointment Number to view records. Click "Generate Digital Bill" to render an elegant, print-ready receipt based on treatment costs.
  5. Exit System: Always click the red "Logout" button at the top right of the dashboard to safely close your secure session.
